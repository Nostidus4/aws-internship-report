### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is friendly, comfortable, and supportive. Team members are willing to help when I have difficulties.

**2. Mentor and Admin Support**  
My mentor gives clear guidance and encourages me to solve problems by myself. The admin team also provides the necessary information and documents.

**3. Relevance to My Major**  
Building DIY Shop's backend (Java/Spring Boot) and frontend (React/TypeScript/Tailwind CSS) built directly on what I studied at university, while deploying it on AWS (VPC, EC2, RDS, S3, IAM, CloudWatch) introduced me to cloud technologies I had only read about before.

**4. Learning Opportunities**  
I improved my technical skills, teamwork, communication, and understanding of professional working processes.

**5. Team Culture**  
The team works seriously while maintaining a positive atmosphere. Members respect and support each other.

**6. Internship Benefits**  
The program provides useful training, practical experience, and flexible support when needed.

### Personal Feedback

The most valuable part of the internship was building a real system end-to-end — from the Spring Boot/React application to the AWS infrastructure running it — and receiving guidance from experienced team members along the way.

For future interns, more team activities and technical sharing sessions could make the experience better.

I would recommend this program to others who want to learn more about cloud technologies and gain practical experience.

### Suggestions

I hope the program continues to provide more hands-on projects, technical workshops, and opportunities to work with team members.
