I participated in building **DIY Shop**, a secure e-commerce platform for a handmade-goods seller, developing the backend with **Java, Spring Boot, and Flyway**, the frontend with **React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS**, and deploying the system on **AWS** (VPC, EC2, RDS, S3, IAM, CloudWatch, Secrets Manager) through a **GitHub Actions CI/CD** pipeline. Through this project, I improved my skills in **backend and frontend development, cloud infrastructure design, secure deployment practices, and technical documentation**.
